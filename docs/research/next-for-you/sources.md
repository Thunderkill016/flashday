# Sources — Mission 008A

Grouped by topic. Links/DOIs for retrieval; no copyrighted text copied.

## Retrieval practice / testing effect

- Adesope, Trevisan & Sundararajan (2017). Rethinking the use of tests:
  meta-analysis of practice testing. *Review of Educational Research*.
  doi:10.3102/0034654316689306
- Rowland (2014). The effect of testing versus restudy on retention:
  meta-analytic review. *Psychological Bulletin* 140(6):1432-1463.
  doi:10.1037/a0037559
- Kang, Gollan & Pashler (2013). Don't just repeat after me: retrieval
  practice better than imitation for foreign vocabulary.
  *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review* 20:1259-1265.
- Barcroft (2007). Effects of opportunities for word retrieval during
  L2 vocabulary learning. *Language Learning* 57:35-56.
- Pretesting/test-potentiated learning: Kornell, Hays & Bjork (2009)
  and subsequent test-before-study literature.

## Spacing / scheduling / memory models

- Kim & Webb (2022). Effects of spaced practice on L2 learning:
  meta-analysis. 98 ES, 48 experiments, N=3411. *Language Learning*.
- Latimier, Peyre & Ramus. A meta-analytic review of the benefit of
  spacing out retrieval practice episodes. g=0.74 spaced vs massed;
  expanding vs uniform g=0.034.
- Nakata (2015). Expanding vs equal spacing for L2 vocabulary.
  *SSLA* 37(4):677-711. doi:10.1017/S0272263114000825
- Nakata (2017). Within-session repeated retrieval effects.
  *SSLA* 39:653-679. doi:10.1017/S0272263116000280
- Nakata, Suzuki & He (2022). Costs/benefits of spacing for L2 vocab;
  relearning override effect.
- Karpicke & Bauernschmidt (2011). Spaced retrieval: absolute spacing
  enhances learning regardless of relative spacing. *JEP:LMC* 37(5).
- Settles & Meeder (2016). A trainable spaced repetition model (HLR/
  Birdbrain). *ACL* 1848-1858. doi:10.18653/v1/P16-1174 —
  +45% recall-prediction error reduction; +12% engagement.
- Tabibian et al. (2019). MEMORIZE: enhancing human learning via spaced
  repetition optimization. *PNAS*. (Optimal-control formulation.)
- Cepeda et al. (2009). Optimizing distributed practice. *Experimental
  Psychology* 56(4):236-246.
- FSRS benchmark: open-spaced-repetition/srs-benchmark; ankitects/
  fsrs-benchmark (DSR model; 727M-review Anki dataset).

## Interleaving

- Nakata & Suzuki (2019). Mixing grammar exercises facilitates
  long-term retention. *Modern Language Journal* 103(3):629-647.
  doi:10.1111/modl.12581 — lower-pretest learners benefit most.
- Pan, Tajran, Lovelett, Osuna & Rickard (2018/2019). Synergistic
  benefits of systematic + random interleaving (L2 conjugation).
- Brunmair & Richter (2019). Similarity matters: meta-analysis of
  interleaved learning. *Psych Bulletin* 145(11):1029-1052.
  doi:10.1037/bul0000209 — overall g=0.42; **words g=-0.39**.
- Working-memory moderator of interleaving on L2 syntax
  proceduralization (LTR 2020).

## Corrective feedback / error handling

- Lyster & Saito (2010). Oral feedback in classroom SLA: meta-analysis.
  *SSLA* — durable effects; prompts > recasts. (Note: Lyster, Saito &
  Sato 2013 is a *review* of classroom CF, not the meta-analysis.)
- Brown (2016). Type and linguistic foci of oral CF: meta-analysis.
  *Language Teaching Research* 20:436-458.
- Ellis, Loewen & Erlam (2006). Implicit vs explicit CF and L2 grammar.
  *SSLA* 28:339-368.
- Fu & Li (2020). Empirical immediate-vs-delayed CF study — immediate
  more facilitative for L2 development.
- Li (2020). What is the ideal time to provide CF? *Language Teaching*
  53:96-108 — replication-agenda/review piece; distinguish from the
  primary empirical studies it replicates (e.g., Li, Zhu & Ellis 2016;
  Arroyo & Yilmaz 2018).
- Quinn & Nakata (2017); Frontiers (2023) systematic review of CF
  timing — immediate-CF theories (SAT, cognitive window ~40s Doughty),
  delay-retention counter-literature.
- Metcalfe & Miele (2014). Hypercorrection of high-confidence errors.
- Kulhavy & Anderson (1972) interference-perseveration hypothesis.

## Input / output / interaction / strands

- Nation (2007). The Four Strands. *Innovation in Language Learning
  and Teaching* 1(1):2-13. doi:10.2167/illt039.0
- Swain (1985/1995). Output hypothesis functions; pushed output.
- Pica, Holliday, Lewis & Morgenthaler (1989). Comprehensible output.
  *SSLA* 11(1):63-90.
- Izumi (2002). Output, input enhancement, noticing. *SSLA* 24(4).
- Task repetition meta-analysis (2025) — fluency consistent, CAF mixed.
- Schmidt — noticing hypothesis.

## Incidental vocabulary / frequency

- Uchihara, Webb & Yanagisawa (2019). Repetition on incidental vocab
  learning: meta-analysis. *Language Learning* 69:559-599.
  doi:10.1111/lang.12343 — r=0.34.
- Webb, Uchihara & Yanagisawa — incidental vocab meta: 9-18% immediate,
  6-17% delayed; modality differences.
- Yanagisawa, Webb & Uchihara — glossing meta-regression.
- Swanborn & de Glopper (1999) — ~15% unknown-word uptake per read.
- Waring & Takaki (2003) — graded-reader incidental learning;
  Nation & Wang (1999) — repetition volume needed.

## Difficulty / cognitive load / challenge

- Bjork — desirable difficulties framework (New Theory of Disuse).
- Guadagnoli & Lee (2004). Challenge Point Framework. *J Motor
  Behavior* 36(2):212-224.
- Wilson et al. (2019). ~85% optimal-error derivation. *Nature Comms*
  10. — narrow (binary classification, SGD assumptions).
- Chen et al. (2018). Undesirable difficulty under high element
  interactivity. *Frontiers in Psychology* 9:1483.
- 2024 comparative review DDF vs CLT (Quarterly J Exp Psych) —
  element-interactivity moderator model.
- Sweller — cognitive load theory.
- Micro-break/vigilance: PMC 2025 micro-breaks study; Guo, Kim & Rubin
  (2014) edX engagement ~6-9min video optimum.

## Adaptive tutoring / ITS / sequencing / KT / RL

- VanLehn (2011). Relative effectiveness of human tutoring vs ITS.
  d=0.79 vs 0.76; granularity plateau. + "What Do Human Tutors Do?"
  (model-scaffold-fade) + "What Makes a Tutorial Event Effective"
  (impasse required; over-helping prevents learning).
- K-12 ITS meta-analysis (2025, arXiv 2511.04997). g=0.271.
- Murray & VanLehn (2006). Decision-theoretic vs fixed-policy tutorial
  action selection. *ITS 2006*.
- 25-year BKT systematic review (Springer 2023).
- JEDM: "When is Deep Learning the Best Approach to Knowledge Tracing?"
  — logistic regression wins on moderate data; DKT needs scale.
- LinUCB sequencing RCT (arXiv 2207.14003, 2022) — completion/
  engagement objective.
- MathBot bandit pace personalization — bandit ≈ randomized-policy
  learning gains, lower exploration cost.
- Offline contextual-bandit ITS policies (arXiv 2606.23015) — Rasch
  proficiency, counterfactual evaluation.
- DABSEC diversity-aware bandits (EDM 2023 poster).

## Assessment / formative / mastery

- Black & Wiliam (1998). Assessment and classroom learning.
- Black — developing the theory of formative assessment (2009);
  "moments of contingency."
- Bloom lineage mastery learning; Whiting high-criterion evidence.
- CAT/IRT item selection: maximum Fisher information; stratified/
  exposure-controlled variants; time-efficient selection modest gains
  (Psychologie Aktuell 2018); CD-CAT DKL/GDI selection rules.
- ALEKS knowledge space theory deployment (J Math Psych 2021).

## Learner factors

- Patall, Cooper & Robinson (2008). Choice effects on intrinsic
  motivation: meta-analysis. *Psych Bulletin* 134(2):270-300.
- Patall, Cooper & Wynn (2010). Choice in classroom — motivation +
  unit-test performance.
- Karich, Burns & Maki (2014). Learner control in ed tech meta —
  motivation yes, cognitive mixed.
- Learner-controlled instruction systematic review — organizational/
  content control work; task control weaker.
- MOOC self-regulated spacing (npj Sci Learning 2020) — spacing
  benefits largest for lower-ability learners.

## AI in L2

- Xu & Wang (2024). AI effectiveness on ELL achievement. *System* 125
  103428 — g=0.812.
- Wu (2024). AI in L2 learning: contextual/instructional/social-
  emotional moderators. *System* 126 103498.
- Lyu, Lai & Guo (2024). Chatbot meta-analysis. *IJAL* 35(2) —
  g=0.608, moderated by mobility/modality/adaptivity.
- Hu (2024). AI-assisted personalized learning meta — moderately
  positive.

## Vietnamese L1 → English

- Ha (2005). Vietnamese-accented English intelligibility; final-sound
  deletion → communication breakdown.
- Schuberg, Masciarelli & Nguyen — final consonant production,
  Colorado State SLA.
- Pham (2025). Coda voiceless stop variants in Vietnamese-accented
  English — 49.3% unreleased/glottalized.
- CTU Journal case study — consonant cluster errors.
- DiVA thesis — English word-final consonants for Vietnamese speakers.
- General EFL grammar-interference literature: articles, tense/aspect,
  word order, plural marking.

## Fluency / reserved

- Task-repetition meta (2025) — the strongest positive evidence for
  a fluency mechanism; still not a calibrated contract.
- McLaughlin automatization/proceduralization lineage.

## Product mechanics (observed, not efficacy evidence)

- Duolingo HLR/Birdbrain (public paper + repo).
- Anki FSRS (open benchmark).
- ALEKS KST (deployment paper).
- Babbel, Busuu, Memrise, Clozemaster, LingQ, Migaku, Language Reactor,

  evidence thin or absent.
